MY BUSINESS ROADMAP

Goals & Vision

A practical direction for my growth as an aspiring businessman.

ROADMAP

From Learner to Entrepreneur

01–02 YEARS

Foundation

  • Complete my studies and strengthen my professional skills.
  • Learn more about entrepreneurship, finance, marketing, and leadership.
  • Build confidence through projects, events, and real-world experiences.
03–05 YEARS

Growth

  • Gain practical business experience and build professional connections.
  • Explore business opportunities and develop a viable business concept.
  • Improve skills in sales, customer service, operations, and financial planning.
07–10 YEARS

Entrepreneurship

  • Establish and grow a sustainable business or business venture.
  • Build a trusted brand with strong customer relationships.
  • Create opportunities for others through responsible and ethical business practices.

VISION

The kind of businessman I aim to become.

I want to become a businessman who combines ambition with discipline, understands customers, treats people with respect, and makes decisions based on long-term value rather than short-term gains.

My long-term vision is to create a business that is profitable, adaptable, and capable of making a positive contribution to the community.

PERSONAL PRINCIPLES

Learn. Connect. Create. Lead.

Every experience is an opportunity to improve, every connection can open a door, and every idea can become valuable when supported by action and discipline.
